A Fair Way Forward: a reliable mobile income-document step

Mobile consumers drop out of the instalment journey at the income document step. A working upload and a clear explanation of why income is asked would let them finish in one sitting without calling an agent.

Practice case: Riverty BU Collection, digital instalment journey. All data comes from a fictional practice data pack and is not Riverty research.

Slide 5 · Strategy

Clear the consumer's way before adding features

Problem hook

Two problems: a journey that breaks on mobile, and one that is unclear and not fitted to the consumer. Only 77.9% continue from the income document step to plan review (all-device figure), and 853 journeys in the sample month asked for a document that never arrived (T-201).

Value proposition

Resolve the biggest bug-related pain points first and review the journey to gain trust and clarity. Clear the consumer's way before adding new features.

Hypothesis

A reliable mobile income-document step for the mobile-first consumer will result in more plans completed in one sitting without an agent call, measured by a lift in 72-hour continuation from document step to plan review. Working target +7.1 points (an assumption). Agent contact within 7 days and missed payments within 60 days are protected.

Slide 6 · Research

The competitor is the manual workaround

Competitive analysis / workaround

The manual workaround is the competitor: the consumer leaves the journey and phones an agent (333 upload-problem and 419 "why is income asked" contacts per month), then repeats details the agent cannot see. The future-state journey replaces this with one sitting, one explanation and shared data. No external competitor is analysed, and the research does not cover one.

PRD · Feature

Reliable mobile income-document step.

PRD · Musts
  • Upload succeeds on mobile
  • Progress and a clear confirmation or error with retry
  • In-flow explainer (purpose, meaning of income, retention period)
  • Answers kept when going back or leaving
PRD · Out of scope
  • Multi-claim plans
  • Pay-day date
  • Chat on every page

Slide 8 · Validation

Experiment plan

Hypothesis

A reliable mobile income-document step for the mobile-first consumer will result in more plans completed in one sitting without an agent call. Working target +7.1 points (an assumption).

Control vs variant

Three-arm A/B test (control, reliability, reliability plus explainer), mobile only, 28 days with enrollment on days 1 to 21.

Primary metric · baseline · MDE

Share of users reaching plan review within 72 hours of first seeing the document step. Baseline: only 77.9% continue from the document step to plan review (all-device figure). Working target +7.1 points (an assumption).

Guardrails

Agent contact within 7 days and missed payments within 60 days, both compared against control.

Ship / iterate / kill

Ship: Ship C if it beats control by at least 5.0 points (p < 0.05) and the guardrails hold.

Iterate and kill rules: see 05-experimentation/experimentation-plan.md
Status

The test has not run yet. Two launch gates are open: compliance wording and retention period, and the mobile-only baseline and traffic.
